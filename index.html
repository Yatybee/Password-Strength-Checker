Forgot your master password?

There is no recovery. Your data is encrypted with a key made from your master password, and nobody (including this app) can decrypt it without it. If you have an encrypted backup you can restore it with the same password. Otherwise the only option is to erase the vault and start over.

    Backups are encrypted with your master password and are safe to store, but a backup is only as safe as that password. Keep one somewhere other than this browser.

    Copying puts the password on your clipboard. Browsers cannot reliably clear the clipboard, so it may stay there until you copy something else.

    Restore from an encrypted backup

    Choose a backup file exported from SecureNest and enter the master password it was made with. The file is decrypted here in your browser to check it is valid. If you already have a vault in this browser, it will be replaced after you confirm.

    Privacy & warning

    Everything runs locally in your browser. No analytics, no external requests, no server. This is an educational prototype that has not had a professional security audit. Do not use it for real, important credentials.
